Compare & verify

Deterministic parsing and diffing over rendered TLF outputs (PDF, Word, RTF) you bring in from your own SAS/R run — SpecGen doesn't execute SAS, so it can't produce these itself, but it can read one back and reconcile it. Sections/tables are matched by their title text, not page position, so re-pagination between two runs doesn't look like everything moved. No model call — nothing leaves this machine.
